# Wheat (Rabi / Winter Season)

Source: BARI Gom-24 sowing-date and fertilizer trial data, via search aggregate of
https://jbau.bau.edu.bd/index.php/home/article/download/133/97 and related BARI
variety trial reporting.
Retrieved: 2026-07-24

- Sowing window trialled for BARI Gom-24: 5 November, 15 November, 25 November,
  5 December, 15 December — i.e. optimum sowing falls in November, with a
  practical outer window into early-mid December.
- Recommended fertilizer dose for BARI Gom-24: Urea, TSP, MoP, Gypsum, Zinc
  sulphate, Boric acid at 115-35-56-5-2-1 kg/ha NPKSZnB (as urea-TSP-MoP-Gypsum-
  ZnSO4-Boric acid respectively).
- Application method: two-thirds of urea plus the entire dose of all other
  fertilizers applied at final land preparation (basal); remaining one-third of
  urea split into two equal top-dressings at 21 and 48 days after sowing, each
  followed by irrigation.

## BAMIS Crop Weather Calendar (12 regions)

Source: BAMIS (Bangladesh Agro-Meteorological Information Portal) — Crop
Weather Calendar of Wheat, covering Sylhet, Rangpur, Rajshahi, Mymensingh,
Khulna, Jashore, Faridpur, Dinajpur, Dhaka, Cumilla, Bogura, and Barishal
regions.
Retrieved: 2026-07-24

- BAMIS-wide crop duration: 119 days, uniform across all 12 regions. This
  directly answers the "no explicit maturity/growth-duration figure was
  found" gap noted below — treat 119 days as the BAMIS calendar figure
  (separate from, and not necessarily reconciled with, the BARI Gom-24 trial
  data above, which didn't report a duration).
- Growth-stage sequence (same order in all regions): sowing → Germination →
  Vegetative Growth → Flowering → Grain Formation → Maturity to Harvesting.
- Regional sowing window: all 12 regions transplant/sow within a similar
  November-start window (a couple of regions, e.g. Rangpur, start in
  October), harvesting by February-March — consistent with the "optimum
  sowing falls in November" guidance already noted above from the BARI trial
  data.

### Favorable weather conditions by growth stage (consistent across all regions with data)

- Sowing: minimum temperature 3-4.5°C, high soil moisture.
- Optimum temperature overall: around 25°C (maximum cardinal temperature
  30-32°C).
- Vegetative growth and flowering: mean daily temperature 15-20°C.
- Maturity: cooler nights, drying soil, bright sunny days favor ripening.
- Daily consumptive water use: 0.17-0.87 (unitless crop coefficient-style
  figure as given in the source) across vegetative growth, flowering, and
  grain formation.
- Phase-wise water requirement across 5 growth phases (sowing/germination,
  vegetative growth, flowering, grain formation, maturity): 47, 128, 77, 137,
  39 mm — total ≈ 428 mm/season, uniform across all regions. This is
  noticeably lower than the rice crops' ~615-621 mm seasonal water
  requirement (see aman_rice.md, aus_rice.md, boro_rice.md), consistent with
  wheat's lower water demand already noted below.

### Congenial weather conditions for pests & diseases

- Leaf rust: temperature 15-25°C, rain and high dew. Listed for every region
  with data.
- Stem borer: max/min/average temperature 31.9-33.9°C / 22.2-26.3°C /
  26.9-29.5°C, average RH ~80%, with rainfall. Listed for every region with
  data.
- Aphid: mean temperature 18.7°C and RH 71.0%, or mean weekly temperature
  29.1°C and RH 55% (two alternative trigger conditions). Listed for every
  region with data.
- Blast (wheat blast, not to be confused with rice blast): continuous rain
  and average temperature 18-20°C during flowering, followed by sunny,
  humid weather; intensifies at 30°C with high RH. Listed for 10 of 12
  regions — absent from Sylhet (the only region whose pest list omits it).
- Loose smut: temperature 22-25°C, RH 60-85%. Listed for Rangpur, Rajshahi,
  Jashore, Faridpur, Dinajpur, Dhaka, and Bogura (7 of 12); absent from
  Sylhet, Mymensingh, and Barishal.
- Black point: rain during ripening. Listed only for Rangpur, Rajshahi,
  Jashore, and Bogura (4 of 12).
- Note: the source table for Khulna has no readable pest/favorable-weather
  text (only embedded images), and Cumilla's pest section appears truncated
  (only Blast and Aphid rows extracted) — treat Khulna as having no BAMIS
  pest data here, and Cumilla's Loose smut/Black point/Leaf rust/Stem borer
  status as unconfirmed rather than genuinely absent.

### Weather warning thresholds (BAMIS, same nationwide)

- Rainfall: >50 mm/day (early season/sowing-germination), >100 mm/day
  (mid and late season) — unlike the rice crops, the threshold does not
  taper back down in late season.
- Wet spell: >50 mm for 3-4 days (early), >75 mm for 3-4 days (mid), >50 mm
  for 3-4 days (late).
- Cloudy weather: flagged as a risk factor in mid- and late-season windows.
- Drought: 20 days without rain, flagged at every stage (not escalating by
  stage the way the rice crops' drought thresholds do).
- High wind: >50 km/hr at every stage.
- Temperature warning: maximum temperature >30°C and minimum temperature
  <15°C, both flagged at every stage.

## Notes for the agent

- Wheat is a Rabi (dry winter) crop — needs supplemental irrigation at sowing and
  at the two top-dressing points, but overall lower water demand than rice.
- Growth duration is now known from BAMIS: 119 days nationwide (see the Crop
  Weather Calendar section above). No yield figure was found in any source
  above; treat yield as unknown rather than inventing a number. Rabi wheat in
  Bangladesh is generally harvested March-April (see cropping_calendar_bd.md),
  which lines up with the BAMIS November-sowing / ~119-day duration figures.
- Unit note: figures are kg/hectare. 1 hectare = 2.471 acres.
