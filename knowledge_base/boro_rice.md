# Boro Rice (Irrigated, Winter-Spring Season)

- BARC-recommended fertilizer dose for Boro rice: Urea 300 kg/ha, TSP (Triple
  Super Phosphate) 112 kg/ha, MoP (Muriate of Potash) 127 kg/ha, Gypsum 75 kg/ha,
  ZnSO4 11 kg/ha.
- For comparison, common (sub-optimal) farmer practice observed in the region is
  roughly Urea 180 kg/ha, TSP 42 kg/ha, MoP 42 kg/ha — noticeably under the
  BARC-recommended dose, especially on TSP and MoP.
- Commonly grown with BRRI dhan29 and BRRI dhan58 varieties.

- Boro rice is planted (transplanted) November-December and harvested March-April,
  overlapping with the Rabi season. (See the BAMIS Crop Weather Calendar section
  below for a more precise, region-by-region breakdown — planting actually spans
  October through January depending on region.)

## BAMIS Crop Weather Calendar (14 regions)

Mymensingh, Khulna, Jashore, Faridpur, Dinajpur, Dhaka, Cumilla, Chattogram,
Bogura, and Barishal regions (14 regions — full national coverage).
Retrieved: 2026-07-24

- BAMIS-wide crop duration: 150-155 days from seedbed to harvest, uniform
  across all 14 regions. This directly answers the "no growth-duration
  figure was found" gap noted below in the original Notes section — Boro is
  the longest-duration rice crop of the three covered in this knowledge base
  (compare Aman rice's 133 days and Aus rice's 126 days, see aman_rice.md and
  aus_rice.md), consistent with it being a long, irrigated dry-season crop.
- Growth-stage sequence (same order in all regions): Seedbed → Transplanting
  → Tillering → Heading → Flowering → Grain filling → Maturity to
  Harvesting. (BAMIS labels this stage "Grain filling" for Boro, versus
  "Grain Formation" in the Aman/Aus calendars — same concept, different
  wording in the source.)
- Regional season windows (by transplanting start, std. week):
  - October-start regions (std. week ~41-43): Sylhet, Faridpur, Dhaka,
    Cumilla — harvest by March.
  - November-start regions (std. week ~46): Rangpur, Rangamati, Mymensingh,
    Dinajpur, Chattogram, Barishal — harvest by April.
  - December-start regions (std. week ~49-51): Rajshahi, Bogura — harvest by
    May.
  - January-start regions (std. week ~3): Khulna, Jashore — harvest by June,
    the latest-finishing Boro regions in the country.

### Favorable weather conditions by growth stage (consistent across all 14 regions)

- Germination: minimum temperature at least 10°C.
- Mid-season (tillering): light intensity ≤200% of normal, high relative
  humidity, soil temperature above 16°C.
- Later season: 22-25°C then 23-27°C temperature windows favor grain filling
  through maturity.
- Phase-wise water requirement across 5 broad growth phases (seedbed,
  transplanting, tillering, heading/flowering, grain filling-maturity): 76,
  120, 190, 145, 100 mm — total ≈ 621 mm/season. This is the same
  water-requirement profile as the Aman and Aus rice BAMIS calendars (same
  national template applied across all three rice seasons) — note that Boro
  is grown almost entirely on irrigation rather than this rainfall-derived
  figure, since the dry winter-spring season supplies little of it as rain.

### Congenial weather conditions for pests & diseases

- Bacterial Leaf Blight: temperature 28-30°C, RH 80-90%, cloudy, rainfall
  >30 mm. Listed for every region.
- Sheath Blight: temperature 28-32°C, high RH, cloudy weather. Listed for
  every region.
- Blast: night temperature 16-20°C for 7.5-8.5 hours (most regions cite 8.30
  hours; Faridpur and Barishal cite 7.30 hours), day temperature 25-30°C for
  the same duration, day-night temperature difference >10°C, RH >90%,
  cloudy. Listed for every region.
- Notably, the Boro calendar lists only these 3 diseases nationwide — none
  of Stem borer, Leaf roller, Rice bug, Sheath rot, or Brown plant hopper
  appear here, unlike the Aman and Aus rice calendars where those are
  universal or near-universal risks. Presence doesn't vary by region for
  Boro the way it does for Aman/Aus — all 14 regions carry the identical
  3-disease list.

### Weather warning thresholds (BAMIS, same nationwide)

- Heavy rain: >50 mm/day (early season), rising to >100 mm/day (mid season),
  back to >50 mm/day (late season).
- Wet spell: >25 mm over 3 days (early), >50 mm over 4 days (mid), 20 mm
  over 4 days (late).
- Cloudy weather: flagged as a risk factor in mid- and late-season windows.
- High wind: >50 km/hr (early), >40 km/hr (mid), >30 km/hr (late).
- Temperature warning: minimum temperature <10°C, flagged at every stage.

## Notes for the agent

- Boro is irrigated rice — it depends on assured irrigation (tube-well/canal), not
  rainfall, since it grows through the dry winter-spring window. Suitable for
  farms with reliable water availability rather than rain-fed-only farms.
- Growth duration is now known from BAMIS: 150-155 days nationwide (see the
  Crop Weather Calendar section above). No per-acre yield figure was found
  in any source above; treat yield as unknown rather than inventing a number
  until a BRRI variety-specific source is added.
- Unit note: source figures are kg/hectare. 1 hectare = 2.471 acres.
- Planting timing should be read regionally, not as a single national date:
  BAMIS shows Boro transplanting starting anywhere from October (Sylhet,
  Faridpur, Dhaka, Cumilla) to January (Khulna, Jashore) depending on
  region — the "November-December" planting window in the bullet above is
  an approximation covering the most common regions, not a fixed rule.
