# Potato (Rabi / Winter Season)

Source: Tuber Crops Research Centre (TCRC), BARI — fertilizer and planting-date
data via search aggregate including
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2405844024076643 and BARI
variety trial reporting (BARI Alu-7, Alu-8, Alu-25, Alu-28, Alu-36, Alu-41).
Retrieved: 2026-07-24

- Planting date: first week of November in the northern region of Bangladesh;
  mid-to-late November in the southern region.
- TCRC/BARI recommended fertilizer dose: Urea 320-350 kg/ha, TSP 220-250 kg/ha,
  MoP 220-250 kg/ha, Gypsum 100-120 kg/ha, ZnSO4 8-10 kg/ha, Boric acid 6-8 kg/ha,
  plus cowdung (organic matter) 5-10 t/ha.
- Growth duration: harvested 90-95 days after planting.
- Yield: mean productivity in trials ranged 38-54 t/ha across varieties and
  conditions.

## BAMIS Crop Weather Calendar (13 regions)

Source: BAMIS (Bangladesh Agro-Meteorological Information Portal) — Crop
Weather Calendar of Potato, covering Sylhet, Rangpur, Rangamati, Rajshahi,
Mymensingh, Khulna, Jashore, Faridpur, Dinajpur, Dhaka, Cumilla, Chattogram,
Bogura, and Barishal regions.
Retrieved: 2026-07-24

- BAMIS-wide crop duration: 105 days from spouting to harvest, in every one of
  the 13 regions — slightly longer than the 90-95 day TCRC figure cited above,
  so treat 90-105 days as the realistic bracket (variety- and
  region-dependent).
- Growth-stage sequence (same order in all regions): Spouting → Seedling →
  vegetative growth → Tuber set/initiation → Tuber bulking/development →
  Maturity → Harvesting.
- Regional planting windows differ by climate:
  - October-start regions (std. week ~41): Sylhet, Rangamati, Cumilla,
    Chattogram, Bogura — harvest by January.
  - Late-October/November-start regions: Rajshahi, Dhaka, Faridpur, Dinajpur
    (std. week ~42-46) — harvest by January-February.
  - November-start regions: Rangpur, Jashore, Khulna, Barishal (std. week
    ~45-46) — harvest by February.
  - Mymensingh starts mid-October (std. week 42), similar to the first group.

### Favorable weather conditions by growth stage (consistent across all regions)

- Vegetative growth (seedling stage): temperature 18-21°C.
- Tuber set/initiation: temperature 10-18°C.
- Tuber bulking/development: day temperature 18-20°C, night temperature
  <15°C.
- Maturity: temperature 15-18°C.
- Sunshine: at least 6-9 hours/day through all stages.
- Relative humidity: 65-80% through all stages.
- Soil temperature: 15-18°C (favorable range, applies around
  planting/sprouting).
- Total seasonal rainfall: 400-600 mm.
- Water requirement: 400-450 mm per growth phase (4 broad phases), i.e.
  roughly 1600-1800 mm of total crop-water demand if irrigation must cover
  the full requirement beyond the 400-600 mm of rainfall.

### Congenial weather conditions for pests & diseases

- Late Blight: temperature 16-20°C; cold, humid weather with drizzle, fog and
  dew accumulation on leaves is congenial — can become epidemic under these
  conditions. Listed in every region; the single most universal potato disease
  risk in the calendar.
- Bacterial wilt: high night temperature 28-30°C and RH 80-90%. Listed for 9
  of 13 regions (Sylhet, Rajshahi, Jashore, Faridpur, Dinajpur, Dhaka,
  Cumilla, Chattogram, Bogura) — not flagged for Rangpur, Rangamati, Khulna,
  or Barishal.
- Fusarium wilt: same trigger as bacterial wilt (night temp 28-30°C, RH
  80-90%) and the same 9-region coverage.
- Potato Leaf Roll Virus (aphid-vectored): average temperature 18-20°C and RH
  ~70% favors the vector. Listed in all 13 regions.
- Termite: foggy, cloudy weather with abnormally high temperature. Listed in
  all 13 regions.
- Potato Wire Worm: soil temperature 10-27°C. Listed in all 13 regions.

### Weather warning thresholds (BAMIS, same nationwide)

- Heavy rainfall: >25 mm/day (early/late season), >100 mm/day (mid-season)
  triggers a warning.
- Wet spell: >25 mm over 2-3 days (early/late season), >50 mm over 3 days
  (mid-season).
- Cloudy weather: flagged as a risk factor in mid- and late-season windows.
- Drought: >10 days without rain (early season) escalating to >15 days
  (mid/late season).
- High winds: >30 km/hr (mid/late season).
- Hail storm: flagged as a risk at any stage.
- Temperature warning: minimum temperature <10°C combined with maximum
  temperature >30°C.

## Notes for the agent

- Potato has the highest fertilizer intensity (by kg/ha) of the crops in this
  knowledge base — factor that into cost estimates.
- Short growth duration (~90-105 days, depending on source) makes it a
  relatively fast-turnaround Rabi crop compared to wheat or Boro rice.
- Unit note: figures are kg/hectare and tonnes/hectare. 1 hectare = 2.471 acres.
- Planting timing should be read regionally: BAMIS gives October starts for
  Sylhet/Rangamati/Cumilla/Chattogram/Bogura/Mymensingh vs. November starts
  for Rangpur/Jashore/Khulna/Barishal, so a single national planting date
  (e.g. "first week of November") is an approximation, not a fixed rule.
- Late Blight, Potato Leaf Roll Virus, Termite, and Potato Wire Worm are
  universal risks (all 13 BAMIS regions); Bacterial wilt and Fusarium wilt are
  regional (absent from Rangpur, Rangamati, Khulna, Barishal calendars).
