# Transplanted Aman Rice (Rain-fed, Monsoon Season)

Source: search aggregate of Aman rice agronomy studies (methodology sections of
field trials), including a nitrogen split-application study —
https://doi.org/10.3390/w14213539 — and transplanting-date trial data.
Retrieved: 2026-07-24

- One field-trial fertilizer rate: Urea 150 kg/ha, TSP 100 kg/ha, MoP 80 kg/ha.
- Another cited rate: Urea 180 kg/ha, TSP 80 kg/ha, MoP 70 kg/ha, Gypsum 60 kg/ha,
  Zinc sulphate 10 kg/ha (180-80-70-60-10 kg/ha NPKSZn formula).
- Urea application method: split into 3 equal parts — one-third at land
  preparation, one-third at 30 days after transplanting, one-third at 45 days
  after transplanting.
- TSP: full dose applied at land preparation (basal).
- MoP: split into 2 equal halves — half at land preparation, half at 30 days
  after transplanting.
- Transplanting window studied: early July through mid-August (01 July, 15 July,
  30 July, 15 August trial dates).
- Typical maturity: approximately 150 days after sowing for common T. Aman
  varieties (varies by variety).

Source: BAMIS / Banglapedia cropping calendar (see cropping_calendar_bd.md)

- Aman season corresponds to Kharif-2: roughly July to mid-October, i.e. the
  monsoon-fed growing window.

## BAMIS Crop Weather Calendar (12 regions)

Source: BAMIS (Bangladesh Agro-Meteorological Information Portal) — Crop
Weather Calendar of Aman Rice, covering Chattogram, Barishal, Cumilla, Dhaka,
Dinajpur, Faridpur, Jashore, Khulna, Mymensingh, Rajshahi, Rangpur, and
Sylhet regions. (This source does not include Rangamati or Bogura — no Aman
data for those two regions should be assumed from it.)
Retrieved: 2026-07-24

- BAMIS-wide crop duration: 133 days from seedbed to harvest, uniform across
  all 12 regions — shorter than the ~150-day generic T. Aman maturity cited
  above, so treat 133-150 days as the realistic bracket (variety- and
  region-dependent).
- Growth-stage sequence (same order in all regions): Seedbed → Transplanting
  → Tillering → Heading → Flowering → Grain Formation → Maturity to
  Harvesting.
- Regional season windows (by transplanting start, std. week):
  - June-start regions (std. week ~23-26): Chattogram, Barishal, Cumilla,
    Dinajpur, Rajshahi, Rangpur — harvest by October-November.
  - Late June/July-start regions: Dhaka (week 25), Faridpur (week 27),
    Jashore/Mymensingh/Sylhet (week 29) — harvest November-December.
  - Khulna starts latest of the 12 (week 30, early July) — harvest extends
    into December.

### Favorable weather conditions by growth stage (consistent across all regions with data)

- Germination: minimum temperature at least 10°C.
- Mid-season (tillering): light intensity ≤200% of normal, high relative
  humidity, soil temperature above 16°C.
- Later season: 22-25°C then 23-27°C temperature windows favor grain
  formation through maturity.
- Phase-wise water requirement across 5 broad growth phases (seedbed,
  transplanting, tillering, heading/flowering, grain formation-maturity): 76,
  120, 190, 145, 100 mm — total ≈ 621 mm/season, uniform nationwide.

### Congenial weather conditions for pests & diseases (uniform trigger values nationwide; presence varies by region)

- Blast: night temperature 16-20°C for 7 hours, day temperature 25-30°C for 7
  hours, day-night temperature difference >10°C, RH >90%, cloudy. Universal
  — listed for every region with data in this source.
- Stem borer: minimum temperature >20.3°C, maximum 29.5-34.7°C, optimum
  24-29°C, morning RH >84%, afternoon RH >38.7%, dry weather. Universal.
- Leaf roller: maximum temperature 32-33°C, RH 92-95%. Universal.
- Rice bug: higher maximum temperature >31.9°C, morning RH >93.9%, sunshine
  >5.9 hrs; alternatively lower minimum temperature <22.1°C, afternoon RH
  <66.4%, dry or intermittent rain, weekly rainfall <40.9 mm. Universal.
- Bacterial Leaf Blight: temperature 28-30°C, RH 80-90%, cloudy, rainfall
  >30 mm. Listed for every region except Barishal.
- Sheath Blight: temperature 28-32°C, high RH, cloudy weather. Listed for
  Cumilla, Dhaka, Jashore, Khulna, Mymensingh, Rajshahi, Rangpur, and Sylhet;
  absent from Barishal, Dinajpur, and Faridpur (and not documented for
  Chattogram — see note below).
- Sheath rot: temperature 25-28°C, cloudy weather, RH >90%. Listed for every
  region except Cumilla.
- Brown plant hopper: temperature >32°C, RH 80-90%, drizzle, wet spell,
  rainfall <75 mm. Listed for Barishal, Cumilla, Dinajpur, Faridpur, Jashore,
  Khulna, Rajshahi, and Rangpur; absent from Dhaka, Mymensingh, and Sylhet.
- Brown spot: temperature 28-30°C, high RH, cloudy weather. Only listed for
  Cumilla and Khulna.
- Gall midge: maximum temperature >33.0°C, afternoon RH <71%, sunshine
  >7.4 hrs/day. Only listed for Jashore.

### Weather warning thresholds (BAMIS, same nationwide)

- Heavy rain: >50 mm/day (early season), rising to >100 mm/day (mid season),
  back to >50 mm/day (late season).
- Wet spell: >25 mm over 3 days (early), >50 mm over 4 days (mid), 20 mm
  over 4 days (late).
- Cloudy weather: flagged as a risk factor in mid- and late-season windows.
- High wind: >50 km/hr (early), >40 km/hr (mid), >30 km/hr (late).
- Temperature warning: minimum temperature <10°C, flagged at every stage.
- Note: the source table for Sylhet ends after its pest/disease list and does
  not include a Weather Warning subsection — the thresholds above are drawn
  from the other 11 regions (which are all identical), so they likely apply
  to Sylhet too but this isn't explicitly confirmed in the source.

## Notes for the agent

- This BAMIS source covers 12 of Bangladesh's Aman agromet regions
  (Chattogram, Barishal, Cumilla, Dhaka, Dinajpur, Faridpur, Jashore, Khulna,
  Mymensingh, Rajshahi, Rangpur, Sylhet). It does not include Rangamati or
  Bogura — don't assume Aman figures for those two regions from this doc.
- Chattogram's entry in the source only has weekly weather normals
  (rainfall/temperature/RH/sunshine/wind); its favorable-weather and
  pest/warning specifics were not present in the source, so they're assumed
  to follow the uniform nationwide template above rather than being
  separately confirmed.
- Blast, Stem borer, Leaf roller, and Rice bug are universal pest/disease
  risks (every region in this source); Bacterial Leaf Blight, Sheath Blight,
  Sheath rot, Brown plant hopper, and Brown spot are regional — see the
  per-pest notes above for which regions flag each one. Gall midge is unique
  to Jashore.
