# Maize (Rabi Season)

Source: search aggregate of BAMIS Package of Practices for maize
(https://www.bamis.gov.bd/en/croppnp/1/all/8/) and BARI hybrid maize Rabi
sowing-date trial reporting.
Retrieved: 2026-07-24

- Rabi sowing window: mid-October to first week of December; commonly cited
  optimum is 15 October-15 November.
- Recommended fertilizer dose (hybrid maize): N 250 kg/ha, P 55 kg/ha, K 110
  kg/ha, S 40 kg/ha, Zn 5 kg/ha, B 1.5 kg/ha.
- Application method: one-third of N (plus the full dose of other nutrients)
  applied as basal; remaining two-thirds of N split into two top-dressings — one
  at floral initiation (knee-height / 8-10 leaf stage), the other about one week
  before silking / grain-filling.
- Yield: BARI hybrid maize trials recorded up to 7.73 t/ha grain yield and 11.4
  t/ha stover yield from a 15 December sowing with BARI hybrid maize 16 — i.e.
  yield is sensitive to sowing date within the Rabi window.

## BAMIS Crop Weather Calendar (14 regions)

Source: BAMIS (Bangladesh Agro-Meteorological Information Portal) — Crop
Weather Calendar of Maize (Rabi), covering Sylhet, Rangpur, Rangamati,
Rajshahi, Mymensingh, Khulna, Jashore, Faridpur, Dinajpur, Dhaka, Cumilla,
Chattogram, Bogura, and Barishal regions — full national coverage.
Retrieved: 2026-07-24

- BAMIS-wide crop duration: 112 days, uniform across all 14 regions.
- Growth-stage sequence (same order in all regions): sowing → Germination →
  Vegetative Growth → Silking & Tasseling → Cob Formation → Maturity to
  Harvesting — a maize-specific staging distinct from the rice crops'
  tillering/heading/flowering sequence and wheat's flowering/grain-formation
  sequence (see aman_rice.md, aus_rice.md, boro_rice.md, wheat.md).
- Regional sowing windows (std. week range): Jashore is the earliest, starting
  September (std. week ~36) — well ahead of the "mid-October to first week
  of December" window cited above. October-start regions (std. week ~42-43):
  Rangpur, Rangamati, Dhaka, Bogura. November-start regions (std. week
  ~45-46): Rajshahi, Dinajpur, Cumilla. December-start regions (std. week
  ~49): Sylhet, Mymensingh, Khulna, Faridpur, Chattogram, Barishal.

### Favorable weather conditions by growth stage (consistent across all regions)

- Germination: minimum temperature at least 10°C.
- Vegetative growth: light intensity ≤200% of normal, high relative
  humidity, soil temperature above 16°C.
- Cob formation: 22-25°C. Maturity: 23-27°C.
- Phase-wise water requirement across 5 growth phases (germination,
  vegetative growth, silking/tasseling, cob formation, maturity): 76, 120,
  190, 145, 100 mm — total ≈ 621 mm/season. This is the exact same
  breakdown used in the potato and rice BAMIS calendars in this knowledge
  base (see potato.md, aman_rice.md, aus_rice.md, boro_rice.md) — BAMIS
  appears to apply one shared favorable-weather/water-requirement template
  across several crops, not a maize-specific figure.

### Congenial weather conditions for pests & diseases

- Seed Rot: threshold temperature 10.9°C and 559 day-degrees C required for
  development; sandy-clay or clay-sand soils suit pupation. Listed in every
  region.
- Fall Army worm: same 10.9°C / 559 day-degree threshold as Seed Rot for
  development; emergence in sandy-clay/clay-sand soils is directly
  proportional to temperature and inversely proportional to humidity; above
  30°C adult wings tend to be deformed; pupae need a 14.6°C / 138 day-degree
  threshold to complete development. Listed in 12 of 14 regions — absent
  from Sylhet and Rangamati (the only two regions whose pest list is Seed
  Rot only).

### Weather warning thresholds (BAMIS, same nationwide)

- Heavy rainfall: >50 mm/day (early season), >100 mm/day (mid-season),
  back to >50 mm/day (late season).
- Wet spell: >25 mm over 3 days (early), >50 mm over 4 days (mid), 20 mm
  over 4 days (late).
- Cloudy weather: flagged as a risk factor in mid- and late-season windows.
- High wind: >50 km/hr (early), >40 km/hr (mid), >30 km/hr (late).
- Temperature warning: minimum temperature <10°C, flagged at every stage.
- These thresholds are identical to the potato BAMIS calendar's weather
  warnings (see potato.md) — again pointing to a shared BAMIS template
  rather than maize-specific figures.

## Notes for the agent

- Maize fertilizer nitrogen requirement (250 kg/ha N) is high relative to rice or
  wheat — factor into cost estimates.
- Sowing-date sensitivity in the source data suggests the season-plan node should
  treat "sow as early as feasible within the Rabi window" as better practice,
  where forecast conditions allow. BAMIS shows some regions (notably Jashore)
  sowing considerably earlier than the "mid-October to first week of
  December" window cited above — read that window as a general guideline,
  not a fixed national rule.
- BAMIS gives a 112-day crop duration nationwide; no separate duration
  figure was found in the BARI trial data above, so this is currently the
  only sourced duration figure for maize in this knowledge base.
- Unit note: figures are kg/hectare and tonnes/hectare. 1 hectare = 2.471 acres.
